About me

Good design starts with listening

I'm Chidinma, a product designer who loves the space between research and craft.

01

I start with people. Interviews, data, and a lot of questions come before a single pixel. The real problem is rarely the one in the brief.

02

I design in the open. I share early sketches, quick prototypes, and honest feedback loops with engineers and stakeholders, so nobody gets surprised at handoff.

03

I sweat the details. Microcopy, edge cases, empty states. The small stuff is what makes a product feel trustworthy.
